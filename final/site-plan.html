Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to present a high-end gourmet restaurant, showcasing elegant dining options, monthly specials, and a menu to entice visitors. It allows users to explore offerings and make table reservations online.

Audience

The intended audience includes affluent food enthusiasts, couples seeking romantic dinners, and professionals looking for upscale business meals. They are likely accessing the site on desktops or mobiles to plan visits.

Dynamic elements

Javascript will be used to add interactivity to the reservation page, including form validation for inputs like date and time, conditional display of available slots based on user selection (using arrays and conditional branching), and DOM manipulation to update confirmation messages. An ES module will organize functions for event listeners on form submission, and array methods like filter will handle time slot availability.
